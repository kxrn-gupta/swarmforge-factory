What is a Software 'Dark Factory'?

In industrial manufacturing, a Dark Factory operates completely automated without human presence on the floor. SwarmForge maps this paradigm directly to software engineering.

🏭 Lights-Out Synthesis

High-level brief in, complete multi-stage software out. The supervisor decomposes work without developer handholding.

Heterogeneous local worker swarms execute independent build waves concurrently with deterministic DAG scheduling.
